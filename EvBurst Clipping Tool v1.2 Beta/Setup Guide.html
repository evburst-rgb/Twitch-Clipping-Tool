Start to Finish Setup Guide

Follow these steps to login, connect your tray app, choose a hotkey, create Twitch clips, and automatically save clips to your PC.

Best for beta users: launch the app first, login on the dashboard, copy your private Trigger URL, then paste it into the tray app using Set Trigger URL.

Setup Steps

1

Launch the App

Open EvBurst Clipping Tool.exe. The app will run in your Windows system tray near the clock. You may not see a normal window immediately.

Look for the EvBurst tray icon in the hidden icons area.
2

Open Dashboard

Right-click the tray icon and choose Open Dashboard. This opens your personal dashboard in your browser.

3

Login with Twitch

Click Login with Twitch and authorize the requested permissions. These permissions allow the app to create clips and post clip links to your Twitch chat.

4

Copy Your Trigger URL

After logging in, copy your private Stream Deck Trigger URL from the dashboard. This URL is your private clip trigger key.

Do not share your Trigger URL. Anyone with this URL could trigger clips on your channel.
5

Set Trigger URL in the Tray App

Right-click the tray icon, choose Set Trigger URL, paste your Trigger URL, and click OK. Your app is now connected to your Twitch clipping account.

6

Choose Your Hotkey

On the dashboard, choose your preferred one-press hotkey and click Save Hotkey.

  • F8
  • F9
  • F10
The tray app must be running for hotkeys to work.
7

Start Clipping

Go live on Twitch, then trigger a clip using one of three options:

Hotkey Press F8, F9, or F10 while live.
Stream Deck Press your Stream Deck Website button.
Tray Menu Right-click the tray icon and select Trigger Clip Now.
8

View & Download Clips

New clips show on your dashboard with title, thumbnail, and open button. The tray app also downloads created clips to your computer automatically.

Videos\Stream Clips
Right-click the tray icon and choose Open Clip Folder to view downloaded clips.

Features Included

  • One-button Stream Deck clipping
  • One-press keyboard hotkey clipping
  • Automatic Twitch chat posting
  • Automatic local MP4 clip downloads
  • Recent Clips dashboard
  • Clip thumbnails and titles
  • Tray app with Open Dashboard and Open Clip Folder
  • Run at Windows Startup option
  • Cloud-hosted Twitch OAuth backend
  • Twitch token auto-refresh support

Important Notes

Your Twitch channel must be live for clip creation to work. If you are offline, Twitch will not create a new clip.

Keep your Trigger URL private. Treat it like a private key for your channel.

Dashboard updates and backend improvements are automatic. EXE updates require downloading the newest release ZIP.
